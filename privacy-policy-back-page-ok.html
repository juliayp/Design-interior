About this portfolio demo

Office Interiors is a Webflow portfolio concept, not an operating interior design business. Business statistics, offers and contact details are illustrative.

The interactive form uses read-only sample values. It does not create accounts, send email, collect passwords or save form data. This demo does not include analytics or set application cookies.

The main page loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers receive browser requests when their resources are loaded. The hosting provider may also process standard access logs.

This page describes the demonstration; it is not a privacy policy for a live business.
